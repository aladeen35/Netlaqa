# SICK NOTE — treatment

Style: Sci-Fi Sitcom Toon (`scifi-toon`). 1080×1920 vertical, 24 fps, ~35 s.
Voice: English (Kokoro TTS). Subtitles: Arabic on top, English below, burned in + `subtitles_ar.srt` / `subtitles_en.srt`.

## 0. Brief (from the user)

> Patient: Doctor, I feel completely exhausted. My whole body aches, and I don't even have the energy for work anymore!
> Doctor: Honestly, my friend… you won't be able to keep up with me! 😂

The joke: the person you go to for rest is more worn out than you. The style's engine (a petty goal, absurd sci-fi means) is used to *prove* the punchline instead of just saying it.

## 1. Three structures

1. **Two-hander with a scanner reversal** — one exam room. The doctor scans the patient (bad), then scans himself (catastrophic): the machine overloads and tears open a portal to a "sleep dimension". Cold pause. The punchline. He falls asleep into the portal.
2. **The waiting-room countdown** — the patient waits while a terminal counts down; every door that opens shows a more exhausted doctor; the last one says the line. Funny, but the dialogue becomes a list and the user's two lines lose their back-and-forth.
3. **Second opinion across worlds** — the doctor opens a portal to consult his alternate selves, each more tired (rule of three → machine-gun), then delivers the line. Strong native move, but it buries the user's punchline under montage and runs past 45 s.

**Chosen: 1.** It keeps the user's dialogue as the spine, it is a pure duo-contrast (panic patient vs. deadpan doctor), and the sci-fi hardware gives the punchline visual proof. The portal appears once, at the peak, as the signature shot. Fits 35 s vertical.

Opening image: not the clinic establishing shot — the patient *flopping face-first* onto the exam bed (sound + motion in the first 2 s).
Ending: callback — the patient, now alone, flops onto the same bed and falls asleep too. Title pops on the flop.
Score shape: a nervous theremin theme → machine-gun beeps → **hard cut to silence** → one soft music sting under the punchline → sleepy lullaby version of the theme on the end card.

## 2. Logline and arc

A worn-out office worker begs a space-clinic doctor for help; the doctor's fatigue scanner rates the patient at 87 % — then explodes when he points it at himself.

- Setup: patient arrives wrecked, complains (user's line 1).
- Turn: the scanner on the doctor overloads → portal to a sleep dimension → silence.
- Ending: "Honestly, my friend… you won't be able to keep up with me!" — he tips back into the portal, asleep. The patient gives up and sleeps too.

## 3. Benchmark

- **Scrubs** (doctor–patient sitcom timing): learn the deadpan reaction holds and the "beat, then the line" rhythm. Don't take characters, sets or jokes.
- **Don Hertzfeldt shorts**: learn how long a silent, still frame can hold before a punchline. Don't take drawing style or text cards.
- Grammar from adult-swim-era sci-fi sitcom cartoons per STYLE.md; no character, colour or prop from any show.

## 4. Cast and worlds

- **Dr. Nodd** (deadpan, low flat voice — Kokoro `am_onyx`, slowed): very tall, lanky, stooped like a question mark. Teal scrubs, an oversized orange surgical cap flopping over one eye, a head-mirror that is a cracked satellite dish. Enormous eye bags (three layers). Absurd idea: a **coffee IV drip** on a wheeled pole that follows him; he sips from its tube.
- **Patient — Gus** (nervous, quick — `am_puck`): short, round, office worker in a hospital gown **still wearing his necktie and clutching a briefcase**. Silhouette: a pear with a briefcase.
- **Clinic palette** (home): sickly mint `#9fd8b8`, beige floor `#d8c7a0`, teal accents `#2e8c86`, terminal amber text.
- **Sleep dimension** (through the portal only): indigo `#2a2370`, lavender `#8a7ae0`, gold stars `#f5c84a`, floating pillows, one snoring pillow creature (non-verbal).
- **Glow colour (portal only)**: hot magenta `#ff3fd2` rim, pale pink core.
- Line `#22172a` 7 px constant, boiling at 12 fps; eye white never changes.
